The Story Behind Zahana

Thoughtfully curated. Beautifully experienced.

Zahana means “the adorned one.”

Founded by the Aarif family, Zahana began with a simple belief: every detail of an event should feel intentional.

What started with a passion for beautifully crafted beverage experiences grew into a broader vision—to create events where design, hospitality, food, entertainment and experience come together naturally.

We believe the most memorable events aren’t necessarily the biggest. They’re the ones where people feel something, where guests feel welcomed, and where the host can actually enjoy the moment.

That’s Zahana.

Every detail considered.
Every guest welcomed.
Every moment beautifully experienced.
